Read before bidding: Grades rate screening attractiveness, not purchase price — plaintiffs credit-bid to their judgment and competition pushes clean deals toward 70–85% of value. Set max bid = 70% of verified value − rehab − $15K friction, and never exceed it. How the grade is set: circuit-court mortgage cases with judgment ≤35% of appraiser value grade A, ≤45% A-, ≤52% B+, ≤60% B; 60–70% is C+ (only works below judgment) and above 70% is D. Judgments under 18% of value (or under $50K) are treated as likely junior-lien/HOA sales and capped at C, under 6% as bait (D); county-court cases are D because the first mortgage survives; tax deeds are capped at B because the opening bid is only the tax debt. Miami-Dade houses take a one-notch penalty (clean SFHs there hammer at ~100% of value) and sales inside 5 days take one more. Duval (Jacksonville) is graded on the same ladder; its benchmarks come from its own auction results. Rehab is estimated at $45/sf of living area. Amber/red rows with tiny judgments are likely junior-lien/HOA sales where senior debt survives. Title search mandatory before any bid — IRS, code-enforcement, and municipal liens are invisible here.
